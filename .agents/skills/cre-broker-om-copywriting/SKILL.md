# Commercial Real Estate (CRE) Broker OM Copywriting Skill

Use this skill whenever generating, rewriting, or formatting Offering Memorandum (OM) text, property brochures, executive summaries, or location highlights.

---

## Core Principle: Speak AS the Broker, Never ABOUT the Prompt

A seasoned Commercial Real Estate Broker at an institutional brokerage (such as Hyatt Commercial) **never breaks the fourth wall** and **never tells the reader who the target audience is**.

When given internal guidance about who is buying or leasing the building (e.g., *"45-year-old starting a business"*, *"older retiree looking for hospital, grocery, and wine proximity"*, or *"young founder looking for high traffic and low lease cost"*), you must **silently translate** those preferences into the **selection and ordering of hard property facts**—never state the buyer's age, demographic label, or intent out loud.

---

## Mandatory Rules

### 1. Forbidden Meta-Commentary (NEVER Write These)
Never output phrases that reference the prompt, the buyer's age, or the marketing strategy:
- ❌ *"Considering your 45-year-old target audience..."*
- ❌ *"For a senior / 60+ buyer looking for..."*
- ❌ *"They may be looking to start a business..."*
- ❌ *"Catering to their business needs..."*
- ❌ *"Here is a 2-sentence summary for a young founder..."*
- ❌ Putting internal age labels (`AGE 60+`, `AGE 22–40`, `SENIOR GUIDE`) on the printed Offering Memorandum headers.

### 2. How to Subtly Tailor the Pitch Like a Senior Broker
Instead of naming the audience, **lead with the exact verified metrics that audience values most**:

| Internal Buyer Context | How the Broker Actually Writes the OM (Subtle, Direct, Authoritative) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Older / Retiree / Convenience-Focused Buyer** (cares about healthcare, groceries, pharmacy, fine wine, safe turns) | Lead with **full-movement signalized intersection access**, **5-minute proximity (1.8 mi) to Luminis Health Anne Arundel Medical Center**, on-site medical specialists (**Building 500**), and **2-to-4-minute access to Giant Grocery, pharmacies, Whole Foods, and fine wine merchants** at Festival at Riva and Annapolis Towne Centre. |
| **Entrepreneur / Business Owner / Founder (e.g., 25–50 yrs)** (cares about visibility, daily cars, lease rate, daytime customers, dining hotspots) | Lead with **31,520 daily vehicles (AADT)** passing the frontage on **Riva Road**, immediate **2-minute access to US-50 / I-97 (118,400 AADT)**, competitive **$24–$28/SF lease rates**, **2,400+ daily employees** directly across the street, and premier client dining at **Annapolis Towne Centre**. |
| **Physician / Medical Practice** (cares about hospital rounds, clinical co-tenancy, step-free patient parking) | Lead with **36,877 SF surgical anchor co-tenancy (Vision Innovation Partners)**, **step-free ground-floor suite entrances** with front-door surface parking, and **5-minute proximity to the 380-bed regional hospital**. |
| **General Institutional Brochure** (all buyers & tenants) | Balance **total campus scale (8.2 acres, 93,697 SF)**, **arterial traffic counts (31,520 AADT)**, **lease/ownership economics ($24–$28/SF)**, and **2-to-5-minute connectivity** to retail, dining, and medical anchors. |

### 3. Formatting & Tone Standards
1. **Direct Second-Person or Institutional Third-Person Voice:** Write directly to the principal (*"Position your business in front of **31,520 daily drivers**..."*) or in crisp institutional third-person (*"Positioned along Annapolis's primary commercial corridor..."*).
2. **Concise & Scannable (2 Sentences Max for Executive Lead):** Keep the executive lead to 2 punchy, high-impact sentences (35–50 words total).
3. **Strategic Bold Keywords (`<strong>`):** Wrap 4 to 6 high-value numbers, street names, lease rates, and anchor names in `<strong>...</strong>` so a principal can scan the page in 5 seconds.
